# MY Scoring Cohort: PDPA 2010 & Amendment Act 2024 Compliance Assessment

## Status

Approved design pattern for data privacy compliance regarding the Malaysian (MY)
market scoring evaluation cohort (`work/2026-08-19-my-scoring-cohort-harness`).

## Purpose & Scope

This document provides a privacy and regulatory assessment under Malaysian law for the
synthetic MY resume cohort used to evaluate AI ranking algorithms, calculate
inter-rater reliability (IRR) metrics (Quadratic Weighted Kappa, Gwet AC2, Fleiss kappa),
and benchmark scoring performance.

Applicable Statutes:
- **Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709)**
- **Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act A1727)**
- **Gazette P.U. (B) 522/2024** (Phased Commencement)

## 1. Statutory Framework & Phased Commencement

The Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act A1727) was gazetted on
17 October 2024, introducing modernized data protection provisions to align Malaysia
with international standards (e.g., GDPR).

The statutory commencement phases under Act A1727 are structured as follows:

| Phase / Commencement Date | Key Provisions / Amendments | Subject Matter |
|---|---|---|
| **1 January 2025** | Section 12A, Section 12B | Mandatory Data Protection Officer (DPO) appointment & Mandatory 72-hour Data Breach Notification to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner (PDP) |
| **1 April 2025** | Section 129 overhaul | Overhaul of cross-border data transfer mechanisms (replacing the whitelist regime with adequacy, contract, or binding corporate rules) |
| **1 June 2025** | Section 43A | Data Portability rights for data subjects |

## 2. Applicability Analysis under Section 2 (Act 709)

### 2.1 Definition of Personal Data

Under Section 4 of Act 709, "personal data" is defined as any information in respect
of commercial transactions that relates directly or indirectly to a data subject, who
is identified or identifiable from that information or from other information in the
possession of the data user.

### 2.2 Fully Synthetic Data vs. Pseudonymized Data

| Data Classification | Characteristics | Scope under PDPA Section 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Real Candidate Data** | Extracted from real job portals, CV submissions, or talent pools | **IN SCOPE** (Full Act 709 obligations: consent, notice, retention, security) |
| **Pseudonymized / Masked Data** | Real resumes with direct identifiers (name, NRIC) stripped or hashed | **IN SCOPE** (Pseudonymization does not exempt data if the individual can still be re-identified via auxiliary data) |
| **100% Fully Synthetic Data** | Generated via deterministic algorithms / LLM slot-filling without real candidate inputs | **OUT OF SCOPE** (Non-personal data: no living data subject exists) |

### 2.3 GDPR Recital 26 International Alignment

International data protection frameworks, notably GDPR Recital 26, establish that
principles of data protection do not apply to anonymous information or to data that
does not relate to an identified or identifiable natural person. Act 709 and Act A1727
embrace this principle: synthetic data generated without referencing or processing
an individual's personal data falls outside the statutory definition of personal data.

## 3. Statutory Obligation Exemption Rationale

Because the N=35 MY scoring cohort is 100% synthetically generated by
`scripts/generate-my-cohort.ts`:

1. **No Consent Requirement (Section 6 Act 709)**:
   There are no natural persons whose consent must be obtained prior to processing,
   scoring, or ranking.
2. **No Notice and Choice Principle (Section 7 Act 709)**:
   No Privacy Notice or disclosure obligations apply.
3. **No DPO Governance Overhead (Phase 1 — 1 Jan 2025, Section 12A)**:
   The dataset does not trigger DPO oversight or data audit logging requirements.
4. **No Breach Notification Exposure (Phase 1 — 1 Jan 2025, Section 12B)**:
   Any leak, exfiltration, or loss of synthetic resume records does not constitute
   a personal data breach reportable to the Commissioner.
5. **Zero Cross-Border Transfer Friction (Phase 2 — 1 Apr 2025, Section 129)**:
   The cohort JSON/CSV artifacts can be safely transferred across regions or analyzed
   in cloud environments without cross-border adequacy assessments or standard contractual clauses.
6. **No Data Portability Requests (Phase 3 — 1 Jun 2025, Section 43A)**:
   No individual can request data portability or subject access regarding synthetic records.

## 4. Synthetic Generation Guardrails & Invariants

To guarantee that the synthetic cohort never drifts into processing real personal data,
`scripts/generate-my-cohort.ts` and its test suite `scripts/generate-my-cohort.test.ts`
enforce the following invariant rules by construction:

1. **Zero Real PII by Construction**:
   - Synthetic name pools derived from common Malaysian naming patterns (Malay, Chinese, Indian).
   - Email addresses strictly restricted to RFC 2606 reserved domains (`@example.com`, `@example.org`).
   - Phone numbers restricted to dummy formatting structures.
   - **Zero National Registration Identity Card (NRIC)** patterns (`YYMMDD-PB-###G` or 12-digit integers).
2. **Generic Company & Entity Descriptors**:
   - Company names use generic structural descriptors (e.g., "Penang Precision Tooling Sdn Bhd", "Klang Logistics Solutions", "Southern Die-Casting Sdn Bhd") rather than proprietary business identifiers.
3. **Automated Zero-PII Test Scan**:
   - CI test `generate-my-cohort.test.ts` scans all generated profiles against regexes for NRIC patterns, real email domains, and identifiable government registration numbers.
4. **No Real Resume Ingestion**:
   - The generation pipeline does not ingest or fine-tune on raw resume databases from Jobstreet, LinkedIn, or Seek MY.

## 5. Residual Privacy Risks & Mitigation

| Residual Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Accidental resemblance to a living individual's work history | Negligible | Purely statistical coincidence; slot-filling combines independent archetype fragments with noise injection. |
| Evaluation label leakage into training | Low | Generator prompts are strictly rubric-blind; verified by automated label-leakage assertions in CI. |
| Ingestion of real candidate data during manual audits | Medium | Human-gated golden-set items (`2026-07-20-my-scoring-cohort-golden-set`) require isolated privacy reviews prior to any real candidate ingestion. |
